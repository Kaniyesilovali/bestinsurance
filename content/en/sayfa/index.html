Before you buy any policy here

EU consumer protection does not apply in the TRNC.

  • The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction here.
  • EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply here.
  • TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes.

In a dispute the body you apply to is the Insurance Arbitration Commission, which sits within KKSRSB (Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans Şirketleri Birliği, the association of insurance and reinsurance companies of Northern Cyprus).

Full methodology

Required by law

Every motor vehicle on the road in the TRNC must carry compulsory third-party insurance. The sums insured differ from those in Türkiye: property damage 1,200,000 ₺, death and injury up to 8 million ₺ on a private vehicle. If the figure you are looking for is a Türkiye figure, it does not apply here.
