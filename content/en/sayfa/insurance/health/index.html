Health insurance in the TRNC

In Northern Cyprus “health insurance” describes two separate things: the Health Fund premium paid to the state for a student residence permit, and a private health policy bought from an insurance company. Neither replaces the other.

Last updated: July 2026 · Sources at the foot of the page

Two separate things

Health Fund premium
Paid to the Ministry of Health. Its basis is Health Fund Law No. 8/2012. It gives access to public health services.
Private health policy
Bought from an insurance company. Its basis is an insurance contract. It covers the private hospitals and treatments named in the policy.
Common mistake

The Health Fund premium is not an insurance policy

In Türkiye, foreign students buy a private health policy for their residence permit. In the TRNC the system is structurally different: under the Health Fund Law No. 8/2012 the student pays a premium to the state. In return the student can use public health services.

There is no policy, no insurance company and no sum insured.

Student residence permit

The student pays the premium in person, not to the university

Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit must pay the Health Fund premium.

If you do not pay

Students who do not pay the premium are charged the full fee tariff at public health institutions.

Read the student residence permit guide

How it is paid

  • 1Through the Student Residence Permit Automation System
  • 2By virtual POS at Kooperatif Merkez Bankası
  • 3Into the Ministry of Health account

The payment is made by the student in person — it is not paid to the university. A medical report is also required for enrolment.

Premium amount

The premium is reported to be calculated as 10% of the minimum wage.

We could not confirm this amount from an official source — for the current figure, ask the Department of Immigration (Muhaceret Dairesi) or your university's student affairs office. When we confirm it, it will appear here.

Work and residence

If you are not a student, the position is less clear

Health insurance is reported to be required in applications for stays longer than three months. However, which type is accepted — the Health Fund premium or a private policy — is not stated clearly in any public source.

For that reason we do not give a definite answer here. We suggest getting written confirmation from the Department of Immigration before you apply; that is better than paying for a policy that is not accepted.

Private insurance

What a private policy adds

In the TRNC, the health products offered by companies are generally grouped under emergency health, personal accident and credit life. Cover and limits vary markedly from company to company — reading the general conditions before buying matters more here than it does in motor insurance.

See the companies that offer health products

The Health Fund gives access to public services. A private policy generally covers:

  • ·Treatment in a private hospital
  • ·Surgery and inpatient treatment
  • ·Outpatient treatment and tests
  • ·Emergency health and personal accident

EU and Republic of Cyprus mechanisms do not apply here

This matters most to readers who arrive from an EU country or expect EU-style protection. TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes, including EU guarantee and compensation schemes. The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC, and EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply here.

The body you apply to in a dispute is the Insurance Arbitration Commission within KKSRSB (Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans Şirketleri Birliği, the association of insurance and reinsurance companies of Northern Cyprus).

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ I have paid the Health Fund premium. Can I go to a private hospital?

The premium gives access to public health services. A private policy is needed for a private hospital.

+ Is my health insurance from Türkiye valid in the TRNC?

Whether a policy issued in Türkiye is valid in the TRNC depends on the policy's geographical scope. Ask your insurance company whether your policy covers the TRNC — do not assume that it does automatically.

+ Is Turkish social security (SGK) valid in the TRNC?

The TRNC operates a separate health system. Confirm with your own institution how your social security registration in Türkiye works in the TRNC.

+ Do I have to pay a premium for my family as well?

The Health Fund premium is tied to the student residence permit. The position of family members depends on the type of residence permit. Ask the Department of Immigration.
