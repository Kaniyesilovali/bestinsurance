Not compulsory

TRNC home insurance

It covers the structure of the home, its contents, or both. An owner and a tenant do not need the same thing: one insures the building, the other insures the contents.

Last updated: July 2026 · Sources at the foot of the page

The short answer on earthquake

There is no compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC equivalent to Türkiye's DASK.

Earthquake is usually offered as one cover item within a home package. If it is not written into your policy, you do not have it.

Frequently asked

There is no compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC

In Türkiye, DASK is compulsory for homes. We could not identify any regulation establishing an equivalent compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC.

What this means in practice: if you have not bought earthquake cover, you do not have it. Do not expect a state umbrella to apply automatically. Check whether earthquake is written into your policy.

Note. Our legislative search did not establish whether any class of insurance other than motor is legally compulsory in the TRNC. So when we say home insurance is “not compulsory”, we are saying that we found no compulsory requirement in the sources available to us. If we find a regulation showing otherwise, we will correct this.

Cover

What is insured

Building (structure)

Walls, roof, installations, fitted cupboards. Usually the owner's policy.

Contents

Furniture, white goods, electronics, personal belongings. Usually the tenant's policy.

Typical covers

  • ·Fire and explosion
  • ·Earthquake
  • ·Flood and internal water damage
  • ·Theft
  • ·Glass breakage

In the product ranges of TRNC companies, home package, glass breakage and theft also appear as separate headings. Whether a cover is included in the package or sold as an add-on varies by company.

If you are a tenant, the owner's policy does not cover your contents. If you are an owner, the tenant's policy does not cover your building.

Before you buy

Four questions before you sign the policy

1Is earthquake cover included?

If it is not written down, it is not there.

2How was the sum insured set?

The cost of rebuilding the home today and the price you paid for it are not the same thing. If the sum insured is too low, the claim may not be paid in full.

3What is the deductible?

The share you pay yourself on every claim.

4Is an unoccupied home excluded?

Policies may restrict cover for holiday homes or homes left empty for long periods.

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ I am a tenant. Do I need insurance?

It is not a legal requirement. But the owner's policy does not cover your contents.

+ Does the state pay for earthquake damage?

We could not identify a compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC equivalent to DASK. How state assistance works is a separate matter, and it does not replace insurance.

+ Where can I read the general conditions?

The KKSRSB general conditions page publishes the fire general conditions. There is no single official general-conditions text covering home packages as a whole. Ask the company for its own policy conditions.

+ My home was bought with a loan. Is insurance compulsory?

Not as a legal requirement, but the finance provider may require it under the contract.
