Travel and border crossing insurance

Two separate subjects on one page: the travel health insurance you buy when leaving the country, and the insurance you must have when you drive to the other side of the border. The second produces far more questions, so we start with it.

Common mistake

The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system

This one sentence answers most of the questions:

  • · Green Card policies issued in Türkiye or in the EU are not valid in the TRNC.
  • · Green Cards are not issued for TRNC-registered vehicles.

So if you are crossing a border with your vehicle — in either direction — you must buy that side's own insurance.

Which situation are you in

Three situations, three separate answers

You are arriving from Türkiye by car

You must take out Temporary Cyprus Motor Insurance (Geçici Kıbrıs Trafik Sigortası) at the point of entry. Your Turkish policy and your Green Card are not valid.

You are crossing from the south to the north (foreign or EU plates)

TRNC compulsory third-party insurance is bought from the "Insurance / Sigorta" kiosks in the buffer zone.

You are crossing to the south with a TRNC-registered vehicle

Your TRNC policy is not valid in the south. Separate insurance is required at the crossing point.

The rule works both ways

Insurance bought in the south is not valid in the north, and insurance bought in the north is not valid in the south. Neither side recognises the other's policy.

Practical detail

What to know before you go to the gate

Only compulsory insurance is sold

Kasko is not sold at the gate. You can buy only compulsory third-party liability insurance. If you are crossing in a rental car, ask the rental company whether the vehicle's comprehensive cover is valid on the other side of the border.

The gate is open 24/7; the insurance desk is not

The crossing points are open at night as well, but insurance is sold only between roughly 09:00 and 17:00 (10:00–18:00 at some gates). If you cross in the evening you may not be able to buy insurance.

Abroad

Leaving the TRNC for another country

Travel health insurance is required for visa applications. What to check when buying a policy:

  • · Geographical scope — a Schengen application generally requires 30,000 € of cover across the whole Schengen area
  • · Where the policy was issued — some consulates raise problems with policies issued in the TRNC; ask the consulate before you apply
  • · Cover for treatment costs, medical evacuation and repatriation of remains

Note. Which policy is accepted for a visa varies by consulate, and there is no central TRNC source that can confirm this. Get the definite answer from the consulate you are applying to.

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ I have a Green Card. Is it valid in the TRNC?

No. The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system.

+ Can I buy kasko at the gate?

No, only compulsory third-party insurance is sold.

+ I am crossing at night. Can I buy insurance?

The gates stay open but insurance sales close in the evening. Plan to cross during the day.

+ Is my TRNC policy valid in the south?

No. A separate policy is required in each direction.

+ Can I cross the border in a rental car?

Rental agreements may restrict this. Ask the rental company first; a separate tariff applies to rental cars at the gate.
