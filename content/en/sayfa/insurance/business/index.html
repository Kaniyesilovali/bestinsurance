TRNC business insurance

Commercial insurance differs from home insurance in two ways: what is insured is not only the building and its contents but also income; and separate liability covers are needed for harm caused to a customer or an employee.

Compulsory cover

Is any insurance compulsory for businesses

Our source search did not establish whether any class of insurance other than motor is legally compulsory in the TRNC.

Among the KKSRSB general conditions there is a text headed “Sertifika Mali Sorumluluk” (certificate financial liability). This points to a product that may be compulsory, but we could not verify its scope or who it is compulsory for.

For that reason we do not say here that “this insurance is compulsory”. Ask your professional chamber, or the Department of Money, Exchange and Development Fund Affairs, whether a requirement specific to your sector exists. This section will be updated when we clarify it.

Your business vehicles are a separate matter

Compulsory third-party motor insurance is required for your business vehicles just as it is for every vehicle.

On commercial vehicles the death and injury limit is higher than on private vehicles: 15,000,000 ₺.

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ I am a tenant. Can I insure my business premises?

Yes. The building is the owner's concern; fixtures and goods are the subject of your policy.

+ Which cover applies if my employee is injured at work?

Employer's liability cover. Third-party liability cover generally does not cover employees — they are two separate policy items.

+ What does business interruption cover do?

It covers the income you lose while you are closed after a loss such as a fire. It is separate from the cover that repairs the building.

+ Are general conditions published for commercial policies?

The KKSRSB general conditions page carries texts such as fire and credit insurance. There is no single official general-conditions text covering commercial packages as a whole. Ask the company for its own policy conditions.
