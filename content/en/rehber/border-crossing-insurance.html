Why

The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system

The Green Card is an international system through which countries recognise each other's compulsory motor insurance. The TRNC is outside that system. This has two consequences:

  • ·A Green Card issued in Türkiye or in the EU is not valid in the TRNC
  • ·Green Cards are not issued for TRNC-registered vehicles

So the two sides of the border do not recognise each other's policies. Insurance bought in the south does not work in the north, and insurance bought in the north does not work in the south.

What to do, direction by direction

Which way are you going

North → south, with a TRNC-registered vehicle

Your TRNC policy is not valid in the south. You must take out separate insurance for the southern side at the crossing point.

South → north, with foreign or EU plates

TRNC compulsory third-party insurance is bought from the “Insurance / Sigorta” kiosk in the buffer zone. The tariff is below.

Türkiye → TRNC, by car (after ferry or flight)

Temporary Cyprus Motor Insurance (Geçici Kıbrıs Trafik Sigortası) is compulsory at the points of entry. Your Turkish policy and your Green Card are not valid.

If you are crossing at Lefke you cannot buy your insurance there. Choose another gate where insurance is sold.

Vehicles do not cross at Ledra Palace or Lokmacı. They are used for pedestrian crossings within Lefkoşa.

Hours

The gate is open 24/7, the insurance desk is not

The crossing points are open at night as well. But insurance is sold only between roughly 09:00 and 17:00; at some gates it may be 10:00–18:00.

If you cross in the evening or at night, you can arrive at the border without being able to buy insurance. Plan to cross during the day.

What is not sold at the gate

Kasko is not sold

The border kiosks sell only compulsory third-party insurance. That means the damage you cause to the other party is covered; damage to your own vehicle is not.

So if you have a single-vehicle accident on the other side, or if you are at fault, the repair of your vehicle is yours to pay for. Check the geographical scope of your own comprehensive cover — it is usually limited to the TRNC.

Travel and border insurance

Rental cars

Ask before crossing in a rental car

Rental agreements may restrict or prohibit border crossings. A separate, shorter-period tariff applies to rental cars at the gate (3 days 20 €, 1 month 35 €).

Before you cross, ask the rental company two things:

  1. 1Does the agreement permit border crossings?
  2. 2Is the vehicle's kasko valid on the other side?

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ I have a Green Card. Is that enough?

No. The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system.

+ Can I buy kasko at the gate?

No, only compulsory third-party insurance.

+ Should I buy an annual policy or buy one each time?

If you cross more than once a month, an annual policy works out cheaper. One month is 25 €, one year 200 €.

+ Can I cross at night?

The gate is open but insurance sales close in the evening.

+ I lost my policy. What happens?

You will not be able to produce it at a check. Photograph it and keep the photograph on your phone.
