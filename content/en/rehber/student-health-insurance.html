Common mistake

The Health Fund premium and a private policy are not the same thing

What you pay is called the Health Fund premium. Its basis is Health Fund Law No. 8/2012. There is no insurance company, no policy and no sum insured.

In Türkiye, foreign students buy a private policy for their residence permit. That model does not apply in the TRNC. Most of the “foreign student health insurance” content you find online describes Türkiye.

Who pays

Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit

Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit is required to pay the Health Fund premium. A medical report is also required for enrolment. The report and the premium are two separate things; both are required.

How it is paid

Payment in three steps

  1. 1Log in to the Student Residence Permit Automation System

    Payment starts here.

  2. 2Pay by virtual POS at Kooperatif Merkez Bankası

    The system directs you to the payment screen.

  3. 3The payment goes to the Ministry of Health account

The premium is paid by the student in person. It is not paid to the university. If your university offers to “handle this for you”, confirm that the payment really reached the Ministry of Health account and that your record was created.

Amount

We do not give a definite figure for the amount

The premium is reported to be calculated as 10% of the minimum wage. We could not confirm this rate from an official source, and the amount changes as the minimum wage changes.

Get the current figure from the Department of Immigration (Muhaceret Dairesi) or from your university's student affairs office. When we confirm it, it will appear here.

If you do not pay

What happens if the premium is not paid

Students who do not pay the premium are charged the full fee tariff at public health institutions. You can still receive treatment, but you pay the full price.

The premium is also part of the residence permit process — not paying it affects your application.

What it covers and what it does not

What the premium buys

Covers

Use of public health services for the year the premium is paid for.

Does not cover

  • ·Private hospitals
  • ·Policy-based private covers
  • ·Treatment abroad

If you want treatment in a private hospital, you must also buy a private health policy. That comes on top of the Health Fund premium, not instead of it.

If you are not a student

Work or residence permits

Health insurance is reported to be required in applications for stays longer than three months. However, which type is accepted — the Health Fund premium or a private policy — is not clear in any public source.

Because we could not close this gap, we are not guessing. Get written confirmation from the Department of Immigration before you apply.

Read this before you rely on EU protection

Your EU health card and EU consumer mechanisms do not work here

  • ·Your European Health Insurance Card does not apply in the TRNC. The TRNC sits outside EU health and consumer mechanisms.
  • ·The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC.
  • ·EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC, and TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes.

If you have a dispute over a private policy, the body you apply to is the Insurance Arbitration Commission within KKSRSB.

Frequently asked

Short answers

+ My university sells health insurance. Should I buy it?

First establish which product it is selling: is it collecting the Health Fund premium, or selling a private policy? The premium is compulsory; a private policy is optional.

+ Is my private health insurance from Türkiye any use?

It depends on the policy's geographical scope. Ask your company whether it covers the TRNC, and confirm with the Department of Immigration whether it is accepted for a residence permit application.

+ Do I pay the premium once?

The premium is valid for the year it is paid for. It has to be renewed each year.

+ Is the premium any use if I go to a private hospital?

No. The premium is for public health services.

+ Does my family have to pay as well?

The Health Fund premium is tied to the student residence permit. The position of family members depends on the type of permit. Ask the Department of Immigration.
