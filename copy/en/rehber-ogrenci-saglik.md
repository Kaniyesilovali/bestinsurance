**Title:** Student health cover and the Health Fund premium in the TRNC | KKTC Sigorta Merkezi
**Meta description:** The Health Fund premium paid for a student residence permit in Northern Cyprus is not private health insurance. How it is paid, what it covers, and what happens if it is not paid.

## Intro

**Eyebrow:** Foreign nationals

**H1:** Health cover for a student residence permit: it is not insurance

**Intro text:**
The most common mistake students make when they come to study in the TRNC is trying to buy a
**private health policy** as they would in Türkiye. The system in the TRNC is different: you
pay a **premium** to the state. That premium is not an insurance policy.

**Sub-note:** Last updated: July 2026

---

## Section — The distinction (at the top, in the warning colour)

**Badge:** Common mistake
**H2:** The Health Fund premium and a private policy are not the same thing

What you pay is called the **Health Fund premium.** Its basis is **Health Fund Law No. 8/2012.**
There is no insurance company, no policy and no sum insured.

| | Health Fund premium | Private health policy |
|---|---|---|
| Paid to | Ministry of Health | Insurance company |
| What it gives | Access to public health services | The covers written in the policy |
| For a residence permit | Compulsory | Does not replace it |
| Document | Payment record | Policy |

**Sub-note:**
In Türkiye, foreign students buy a private policy for their residence permit. **That model does
not apply in the TRNC.** Most of the "foreign student health insurance" content you find online
describes Türkiye.

---

## Section — Who pays

**H2:** Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit

Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit is required to pay the
Health Fund premium.

A **medical report** is also required for enrolment. The report and the premium are two separate
things; both are required.

---

## Section — How it is paid (step by step)

**H2:** Payment in three steps

**1. Log in to the Student Residence Permit Automation System**
Payment starts here.

**2. Pay by virtual POS at Kooperatif Merkez Bankası**
The system directs you to the payment screen.

**3. The payment goes to the Ministry of Health account**

**Emphasis box:**
> The premium is paid **by the student in person. It is not paid to the university.**
> If your university offers to "handle this for you", confirm that the payment really reached
> the Ministry of Health account and that your record was created.

## Section — Amount (the honesty section)

**H2:** We do not give a definite figure for the amount

The premium is reported to be calculated as **10% of the minimum wage**. We could not confirm
this rate from an official source, and the amount changes as the minimum wage changes.

Get the current figure from the **Department of Immigration** (Muhaceret Dairesi) or from your
university's student affairs office. When we confirm it, it will appear here.

---

## Section — If you do not pay

**H2:** What happens if the premium is not paid

Students who do not pay the premium are charged the **full fee tariff** at public health
institutions. You can still receive treatment, but you pay the full price.

The premium is also part of the residence permit process — not paying it affects your
application.

---

## Section — What it covers and what it does not

**H2:** What the premium buys

**Covers**
Use of **public** health services for the year the premium is paid for.

**Does not cover**
- Private hospitals
- Policy-based private covers
- Treatment abroad

If you want treatment in a private hospital, you must also buy a **private health policy**.
That comes on top of the Health Fund premium, not instead of it.

## Section — If you are not a student

**H2:** Work or residence permits

Health insurance is reported to be required in applications for stays longer than three months.
However, **which type is accepted — the Health Fund premium or a private policy — is not clear
in any public source.**

Because we could not close this gap, we are not guessing. Get written confirmation from the
Department of Immigration before you apply.

---

## Section — Read this before you rely on EU protection

**H2:** Your EU health card and EU consumer mechanisms do not work here

- **Your European Health Insurance Card does not apply in the TRNC.** The TRNC sits outside EU
  health and consumer mechanisms.
- **The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC.**
- **EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC**, and TRNC insurance companies are
  not covered by EU insurance schemes.

If you have a dispute over a private policy, the body you apply to is the Insurance Arbitration
Commission within KKSRSB.

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**My university sells health insurance. Should I buy it?**
First establish which product it is selling: is it collecting the Health Fund premium, or
selling a private policy? The premium is compulsory; a private policy is optional.

**Is my private health insurance from Türkiye any use?**
It depends on the policy's geographical scope. Ask your company whether it covers the TRNC, and
confirm with the Department of Immigration whether it is accepted for a residence permit
application.

**Do I pay the premium once?**
The premium is valid for the year it is paid for. It has to be renewed each year.

**Is the premium any use if I go to a private hospital?**
No. The premium is for public health services.

**Does my family have to pay as well?**
The Health Fund premium is tied to the student residence permit. The position of family members
depends on the type of permit. Ask the Department of Immigration.
