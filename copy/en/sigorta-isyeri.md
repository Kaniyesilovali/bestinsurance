## Intro

**Badge:** Not compulsory

**H1:** TRNC business insurance

**Intro text:**
Commercial insurance differs from home insurance in two ways: what is insured is not only the
building and its contents but also **income**; and separate liability covers are needed for
harm caused to a customer or an employee.

## Section — Compulsory cover

**H2:** Is any insurance compulsory for businesses

Our source search did not establish whether any class of insurance other than motor is legally
compulsory in the TRNC.

Among the KKSRSB general conditions there is a text headed **"Sertifika Mali Sorumluluk"**
(certificate financial liability). This points to a product that may be compulsory, but we
could not verify its scope or who it is compulsory for.

**For that reason we do not say here that "this insurance is compulsory".** Ask your
professional chamber, or the Department of Money, Exchange and Development Fund Affairs,
whether a requirement specific to your sector exists. This section will be updated when we
clarify it.

**Separate note:**
Compulsory third-party motor insurance is required for your business vehicles just as it is
for every vehicle. On commercial vehicles the death and injury limit is higher than on private
vehicles: **15,000,000 ₺.**

## Section — Before you buy

**H2:** Four things before you ask for a quotation

1. **Calculate the sum insured at today's value.** If stock and fixtures are declared from an
   outdated list, the claim will be underpaid.
2. **Check the period of the business interruption cover.** How many months of lost income are
   covered?
3. **Look at the liability limit.** Is the policy limit enough if a customer is injured?
4. **Establish who is included.** Employees, subcontractors, visitors — they are not all under
   the same cover.

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**I am a tenant. Can I insure my business premises?**
Yes. The building is the owner's concern; fixtures and goods are the subject of your policy.

**Which cover applies if my employee is injured at work?**
Employer's liability cover. Third-party liability cover generally does not cover employees —
they are two separate policy items.

**What does business interruption cover do?**
It covers the income you lose while you are closed after a loss such as a fire. It is separate
from the cover that repairs the building.

**Are general conditions published for commercial policies?**
The KKSRSB general conditions page carries texts such as fire and credit insurance. There is
no single official general-conditions text covering commercial packages as a whole. Ask the
company for its own policy conditions.
