## Intro

**Eyebrow:** Border crossing

**H1:** Driving to the other side of the border

**Intro text:**
The answer is short: **your policy is not valid on the other side.** Whichever direction you
cross in, you must buy the insurance of the side you are crossing into. The rest is the
practical detail.

**Sub-note:** Last updated: July 2026

---

## Section — Why

**H2:** The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system

The Green Card is an international system through which countries recognise each other's
compulsory motor insurance. **The TRNC is outside that system.** This has two consequences:

- A Green Card issued in Türkiye or in the EU is **not valid in the TRNC**
- **Green Cards are not issued for TRNC-registered vehicles**

So the two sides of the border do not recognise each other's policies. Insurance bought in the
south does not work in the north, and insurance bought in the north does not work in the south.

---

## Section — What to do, direction by direction

**H2:** Which way are you going

**North → south, with a TRNC-registered vehicle**
Your TRNC policy is not valid in the south. You must take out separate insurance for the
southern side at the crossing point.

**South → north, with foreign or EU plates**
TRNC compulsory third-party insurance is bought from the **"Insurance / Sigorta"** kiosk in the
buffer zone. The tariff is below.

**Türkiye → TRNC, by car (after ferry or flight)**
**Temporary Cyprus Motor Insurance** (Geçici Kıbrıs Trafik Sigortası) is compulsory at the
points of entry. Your Turkish policy and your Green Card are not valid.

| Strovilia | Yes | Yes |
| Güzelyurt (Astromeritis / Zodhia) | Yes | Yes |
| Pirgos | Yes | Yes |
| **Lefke** | Yes | **No** |
| Ledra Palace | **No** — pedestrians and bicycles | — |
| Lokmacı | **No** — pedestrians and bicycles | — |

**Two warnings below the table:**

**If you are crossing at Lefke** you cannot buy your insurance there. Choose another gate where
insurance is sold.

**Vehicles do not cross at Ledra Palace or Lokmacı.** They are used for pedestrian crossings
within Lefkoşa.

## Section — Hours (the detail that causes the most trouble)

**H2:** The gate is open 24/7, the insurance desk is not

The crossing points are open at night as well. But **insurance is sold only between roughly
09:00 and 17:00**; at some gates it may be 10:00–18:00.

If you cross in the evening or at night, you can arrive at the border without being able to buy
insurance. **Plan to cross during the day.**

**Below the table:**
Fees are in euro and were compiled from a commercial source — the current amount at the gate may
differ. If you cross often, an annual policy is markedly cheaper than a monthly one: one month
is 25 €, twelve months 200 €.

## Section — What is not sold at the gate

**H2:** Kasko is not sold

The border kiosks sell **only compulsory third-party insurance.** That means the damage you
cause to the other party is covered; **damage to your own vehicle is not.**

So if you have a single-vehicle accident on the other side, or if you are at fault, the repair
of your vehicle is yours to pay for. Check the geographical scope of your own kasko — it is
usually limited to the TRNC.

---

## Section — Rental cars

**H2:** Ask before crossing in a rental car

Rental agreements may restrict or prohibit border crossings. A separate, shorter-period tariff
applies to rental cars at the gate (3 days 20 €, 1 month 35 €).

Before you cross, ask the rental company two things:
1. Does the agreement permit border crossings?
2. Is the vehicle's kasko valid on the other side?

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**I have a Green Card. Is that enough?**
No. The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system.

**Can I buy kasko at the gate?**
No, only compulsory third-party insurance.

**Should I buy an annual policy or buy one each time?**
If you cross more than once a month, an annual policy works out cheaper. One month is 25 €, one
year 200 €.

**Can I cross at night?**
The gate is open but insurance sales close in the evening.

**I lost my policy. What happens?**
You will not be able to produce it at a check. Photograph it and keep the photograph on your
phone.
