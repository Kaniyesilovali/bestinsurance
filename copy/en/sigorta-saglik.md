## Intro

**H1:** Health insurance in the TRNC

**Intro text:**
In Northern Cyprus "health insurance" describes two separate things: the **Health Fund
premium** paid to the state for a student residence permit, and a **private health policy**
bought from an insurance company. Neither replaces the other.

**Sub-note:** Last updated: July 2026 · Sources at the foot of the page

## Section — The critical distinction (top of the page, in the warning colour)

**Badge:** Common mistake
**H2:** The Health Fund premium is not an insurance policy

In Türkiye, foreign students buy a **private health policy** for their residence permit. In
the TRNC the system is structurally different: under the **Health Fund Law No. 8/2012** the
student pays a premium to the state. In return the student can use **public** health services.

There is no policy, no insurance company and no sum insured.

| | Health Fund premium | Private health policy |
|---|---|---|
| Paid to | Ministry of Health | Insurance company |
| Basis | Health Fund Law No. 8/2012 | Insurance contract |
| Cover | Public health services | The private hospitals and treatments named in the policy |
| For a residence permit | Compulsory for students | Does not replace it |
| Sum insured | None — access to a service | Stated in the policy |

## Section — Students

**Eyebrow:** Student residence permit
**H2:** The student pays the premium in person, not to the university

Every foreign national student applying for a student residence permit must pay the Health
Fund premium.

**How it is paid**
1. Through the Student Residence Permit Automation System
2. By virtual POS at Kooperatif Merkez Bankası
3. Into the Ministry of Health account

The payment is made **by the student in person** — it is not paid to the university. A medical
report is also required for enrolment.

**If you do not pay**
Students who do not pay the premium are charged the **full fee tariff** at public health
institutions.

**Premium amount box:**
The premium is reported to be calculated as **10% of the minimum wage**.
*We could not confirm this amount from an official source. For the current figure, ask the
Department of Immigration (Muhaceret Dairesi) or your university's student affairs office.
When we confirm it, it will appear here.*

## Section — Other foreign nationals

**Eyebrow:** Work and residence
**H2:** If you are not a student, the position is less clear

Health insurance is reported to be required in applications for stays longer than three
months. However, **which type is accepted — the Health Fund premium or a private policy — is
not stated clearly in any public source.**

For that reason we do not give a definite answer here. We suggest getting written confirmation
from the Department of Immigration before you apply. That is better than paying for a policy
that is not accepted.

## Section — Private health policy

**Eyebrow:** Private insurance
**H2:** What a private policy adds

The Health Fund gives access to public services. A private policy generally covers:

- Treatment in a private hospital
- Surgery and inpatient treatment
- Outpatient treatment and tests
- Emergency health and personal accident

In the TRNC, the health products offered by companies are generally grouped under emergency
health, personal accident and credit life. Cover and limits vary markedly from company to
company. Reading the general conditions before buying matters more here than it does in motor
insurance.

## Section — Read this before you rely on EU protection

**H2:** EU and Republic of Cyprus consumer mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC

This matters most to readers who arrive from an EU country or expect EU-style protection.

- **The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC.**
- **EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC.**
- **TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes**, including EU
  guarantee and compensation schemes.

The body you apply to in a dispute is the Insurance Arbitration Commission within KKSRSB
(Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans Şirketleri Birliği, the association of insurance and
reinsurance companies of Northern Cyprus).

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**I have paid the Health Fund premium. Can I go to a private hospital?**
The premium gives access to public health services. A private policy is needed for a private
hospital.

**Is my health insurance from Türkiye valid in the TRNC?**
Whether a policy issued in Türkiye is valid in the TRNC depends on the policy's geographical
scope. Ask your insurance company whether your policy covers the TRNC. Do not assume that it
does automatically.

**Is Turkish social security (SGK) valid in the TRNC?**
The TRNC operates a separate health system. Confirm with your own institution how your social
security registration in Türkiye works in the TRNC.

**Do I have to pay a premium for my family as well?**
The Health Fund premium is tied to the student residence permit. The position of family
members depends on the type of residence permit. Ask the Department of Immigration.
