## Intro

**Badge:** Not compulsory

**H1:** TRNC comprehensive motor insurance (kasko)

**Intro text:**
Comprehensive motor insurance (kasko) covers damage to your own vehicle. Compulsory
third-party motor insurance pays only for the damage you cause to the other party. These
are two separate policies. Neither replaces the other.

**Sub-note:** Last updated: July 2026 · Sources at the foot of the page

**Side box — the distinction in one sentence:**
> If the accident was your fault, only kasko repairs your vehicle.

## Section — The difference between the two policies

**Eyebrow:** The confusion
**H2:** Having compulsory motor insurance does not mean your vehicle is insured

Comparison table:

| | Compulsory third-party | Kasko |
|---|---|---|
| Legal status | Compulsory for every vehicle | Optional |
| Whose loss | The other party's | Your own vehicle's |
| If you are at fault, your vehicle | Not paid | Paid |
| Legal basis | Chapter 333 | Contract; general conditions held by KKSRSB |
| Pricing regime | Floor tariff | Floor loading (minimum premium) |

**Below the table:**
This is the most common misunderstanding in the TRNC. The compulsory policy protects you
from a penalty. It does not protect your vehicle.

## Section — Cover

**Eyebrow:** Cover
**H2:** What it covers and what it does not

**Covers**
- Collision and overturning — even where you are at fault
- Fire and explosion
- Theft and damage caused during an attempted theft
- Natural disasters
- Glass breakage — may be a separate cover depending on the policy

**Does not cover**
- Damage you cause to the other party — compulsory third-party motor insurance is needed for that
- Driving without a licence
- Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Racing and test driving

**Note below the box:**
Which covers are standard in the policy and which are add-ons varies by company.
KKSRSB publishes the kasko general conditions in Turkish and English. Both are official
texts, not translations. English readers should use the English text directly.

## Section — Price

**Eyebrow:** Pricing
**H2:** Kasko has a floor as well

As with compulsory motor insurance, a minimum premium is set for kasko. The sector report
calls this **"taban çarpma"** (floor loading), and it is banded by vehicle value.

**2025 kasko floor premium**

| Vehicle value | Floor premium |
|---|---|
| Up to 95,000 ₺ | 6,000 ₺ |
| Up to 185,000 ₺ | 8,000 ₺ |

**How much it rose in one year — box:**
In 2024 it was 4,008.90 ₺ for a vehicle worth up to 60,135 ₺, and 6,013.35 ₺ for a vehicle
worth up to 120,267 ₺. Both the premium and the vehicle-value ceiling of each band went up.

**Verification note:**
These figures come from the Insurance Supervisor's 2024 Insurance Sector Report and are the
**2025** tariff. The 2026 tariff has not been published yet. This section will be updated
when it is.

**Market observation box:**
A range of 300–500 € a year is reported for full kasko.
*This figure comes from a commercial source, not official. It varies with the value and age
of the vehicle and the scope of cover.*

## Section — How to compare

**H2:** Kasko prices do differ, but price is not the only measure

In compulsory motor insurance the floor tariff pushes prices close together. In kasko the
scope of cover varies from company to company, so two policies at the same price can cover
very different things.

When comparing, look at:
- Whether glass, towing and a replacement vehicle are included or sold as add-ons
- The size of the deductible (the share you pay yourself)
- How you report a claim — phone, WhatsApp, app
- How long the loss adjuster takes to arrive

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**Is kasko compulsory?**
No. In the TRNC only third-party liability insurance (compulsory motor insurance) is a legal
requirement. However, if you bought the vehicle on credit or lease, the finance provider may
require kasko under the contract.

**The accident was the other driver's fault. Should I use my kasko?**
The other party's compulsory motor insurance covers the repair of your vehicle. If you use
your own kasko, your no-claims step may be affected. A step system is applied in the TRNC and
the records are held at Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta Bilgi Merkezi (KKSBM, the North Cyprus Insurance
Information Centre). However, **the number of steps and the discount rates are not published
in any public source**, so we do not give a rate here. Ask the company that issued your policy.

**Can I buy kasko at a border crossing?**
No. The insurance kiosks in the buffer zone sell only compulsory third-party insurance. Kasko
is not sold there.

**Where do I go if there is a dispute about a kasko claim?**
You can apply to the Insurance Arbitration Commission, which sits within KKSRSB, using its
application form. The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman and EU consumer mechanisms do
not apply in the TRNC.

**Where can I read the kasko general conditions?**
KKSRSB publishes the kasko general conditions in Turkish and English. The English version is
an official text, not a translation.
