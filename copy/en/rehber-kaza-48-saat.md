## Section — Ordered list (at the very top of the page, numbered)

**H2:** At the scene

**1. Do not move the vehicle. Make the scene safe**
If anyone is injured, call an ambulance first. Unless the vehicles are creating a danger to
traffic, leave them where they are.

**2. Call the police**
In the TRNC the **police accident report** is the basic document in a claim file. Progressing a
file without one is difficult. Even if the other party says "let us settle this between us",
have a report made.

**3. Take photographs**
- The damaged areas of both vehicles, close up and from a distance
- The position of the vehicles on the road
- Number plates
- The road, signs, and any skid marks
- Anything else that was damaged: wall, kerb, pole

**4. Take the other party's details**
Full name, telephone, number plate, **insurance company and policy number.** If the driver is
not the owner of the vehicle, take the details of both.

**5. If there are witnesses, take their contact details**
They are very hard to find later.

---

## Section — The next 48 hours

**H2:** After you leave the scene

**6. Notify your insurance company**
Call without delay. Have your policy number, the place and time of the accident, and the police
report details ready.

**7. Book the loss adjuster**
Do not put the vehicle in for repair before it has been inspected. Once it is repaired, the
extent of the damage is harder to document.

**8. Keep the documents together**
Police report, photographs, adjuster's report, invoices, and a doctor's report if anyone was
injured.

## Section — Notification deadline (the honesty section)

**H2:** We do not give a figure for the deadline

We could not establish a standard TRNC-specific deadline for how many days you have to report
an accident. Most of the figures that come up online for this question relate to practice in
Türkiye and do not apply in the TRNC.

**The practical answer:** the deadline is written in the general conditions of your policy and
may vary by company. Take out your policy and read it. The safest course is to report on the
day of the accident.

When we confirm it from a source, it will appear here.

## Section — If the fault is not yours

**H2:** Which policy pays when the other party is at fault

The other party's **compulsory third-party motor insurance** covers the repair of your vehicle.
You do not need to use your own kasko for this.

If you do use your own kasko, your no-claims step may be affected. A step system is applied in
the TRNC and the records are held at Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta Bilgi Merkezi (KKSBM, the North
Cyprus Insurance Information Centre). However, because the number of steps and the discount
rates are not published in any public source, we do not give a rate here. Ask your company.

## Section — If the other party is uninsured

**H2:** The Guarantee Fund steps in

The **Guarantee Fund**, which sits within KKSRSB (Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans Şirketleri
Birliği, the association of insurance and reinsurance companies of Northern Cyprus), covers
losses caused by:

- **Uninsured** vehicles
- **Unlicensed** drivers
- **Hit-and-run** vehicles

**Emphasis box:**
> Applications are made through a lawyer, and **the lawyer's fee is met by the Fund.**
> There is no cost to the applicant.

**Documents required**
- Police accident report
- Photographs of the damage
- Vehicle damage assessment
- Doctor's report if anyone was injured
- Invoices relating to the damage

**Note:**
We could not verify the Fund's payment limits or the time limit for applying. Ask the Fund
before you apply — waiting may work against you.

## Section — If your claim is rejected

**H2:** A five-step route

1. **Written objection to the company.** Ask for the reason for rejection in writing. Do not
   accept a verbal answer.
2. **KKSRSB Insurance Arbitration Commission.** Applications are made using the application
   form. The Commission operates within KKSRSB.
3. **Guarantee Fund** — if the other party was uninsured, unlicensed or drove off.
4. **Department of Money, Exchange and Development Fund Affairs** — a regulatory complaint
   about the company's conduct.
5. **The courts.**

**Warning box:**
We could not verify the Arbitration Commission's monetary application limits, its application
fee, its decision period, or whether its decisions are binding. Get confirmation from the
Commission before you apply.

## Section — Read this before you rely on EU protection

**H2:** EU and Republic of Cyprus consumer mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC

If you are used to EU consumer protection, this is the most important paragraph on the page.

- **The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC.**
- **EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC.**
- **TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes.**

The five steps above are the whole of the route available to you.

## Section — Conclusion / link to choosing a company

**H2:** This is why we look at accessibility when choosing a company

When you have a claim, it is not the company's price that helps you but whether you can
**reach** it: whether the phone is answered, whether there is a WhatsApp line, whether the
office is near you, how soon the loss adjuster can come.

Of the 39 companies, only three are headquartered outside Lefkoşa. If you live in Girne or
Gazimağusa, that may matter more than the price of the policy.
