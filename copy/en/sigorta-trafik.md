## Intro

**Badge:** Legal requirement

**H1:** TRNC compulsory motor insurance

**Intro text:**
Every motor vehicle used on the road in Northern Cyprus must hold this policy. It pays for
the damage you cause to other people. It does not cover your own vehicle. The legal basis is
Chapter 333, the Motor Vehicles (Third Party Insurance) Law.

**Sub-note:** Last updated: July 2026 · Sources at the foot of the page

**Side box — in one sentence:**
> This policy protects you from a penalty and protects other people. It does not protect your car.

## Section — What does not carry over

**Eyebrow:** The common mistake
**H2:** Your home country's arrangements do not apply here

This is the block that replaces the Turkey comparison on the Turkish page.

- **A green card does not work.** The TRNC is not a member of the green card system. Green
  cards issued in the EU or in Turkey are not valid here, and no green card is issued for a
  TRNC-registered vehicle.
- **EU consumer mechanisms do not apply.** TRNC insurers are not within EU insurance schemes.
- **The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction here.** A complaint about a
  TRNC policy cannot be taken to it.
- **A policy bought in another country does not cover a TRNC-registered vehicle.** If you have
  bought a vehicle here, you need a policy from a company licensed here.

**Where complaints do go:**
The Insurance Arbitration Commission sits within KKSRSB (Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans
Şirketleri Birliği, the North Cyprus Association of Insurance and Reinsurance Companies) and
accepts applications through its own form.

## Section — Cover

**Eyebrow:** Cover
**H2:** What it covers and what it does not

**Covers**
- Repair of the other party's vehicle
- Towing costs
- Death or permanent disability of third parties
- Emergency medical treatment costs
- Other property damage caused to third parties — walls, kerbs, electricity poles

**Does not cover**
- Damage to your own vehicle — comprehensive motor insurance (kasko) is needed for that
- Damage arising during racing or test driving
- Driving without a licence
- Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs

**Note below the box:**
The full list of exclusions is in the policy general conditions. KKSRSB publishes these in
Turkish and English. Both are official texts, not translations — read the English one directly.

## Section — Pricing

**Eyebrow:** Pricing
**H2:** Prices are not free to move

A minimum premium is set by vehicle use class and **no company may quote below that floor**.
Companies must notify the Department of Money, Exchange and Development Fund Affairs and
Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta Bilgi Merkezi (KKSBM, the North Cyprus Insurance Information Centre) at
least 10 days before applying a new tariff.

What this means in practice: **shopping for the cheapest quote achieves little.** The
difference between companies shows up in service, not in price.

**2025 floor tariff — from 20 March 2025**

| Vehicle use class | Floor 1 | Floor 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Saloon car (CX1) | 4,000 | 5,000 |
| Motorcycle (CY1) | 2,000 | 2,500 |
| Van / pickup (CZ 300) | 4,000 | 5,000 |
| Lorry (CZ 301) | 5,000 | 5,500 |
| Trailer (CZ 312) | 2,200 | 2,500 |
| Taxi (CZ 400) | 11,500 | 13,500 |
| Bus / minibus, commercial (CZ 600) | 8,500 | 10,000 |
| Bus / minibus (CZ 605) | 7,500 | 8,500 |
| Motor trade (CZ 9) | 8,000 | 9,500 |
| Special-type vehicles (CZ 801) | 4,000 | 4,500 |
| Ambulance and hearse (CZ 803) | 4,500 | 5,000 |
| Cranes (CZ 804) | 5,000 | 6,000 |

Amounts in Turkish lira.

**Verification note:**
Taken from the Insurance Supervisor's 2024 Sector Report. Four rows whose codes were illegible
were left out — the text layer of the source PDF is partly corrupted. The 2026 tariff has not
been published; this section will be updated when it is.

**No-claims discount:**
A bonus-malus (step) system operates in the TRNC and records are held at KKSBM. **The number
of steps and the discount rates are not published in any public source**, so no rate is given
here. Rates from other countries do not apply.

## Section — If an uninsured vehicle hits you

**Eyebrow:** Guarantee Fund

The Guarantee Fund within KKSRSB covers loss caused by **uninsured vehicles, unlicensed
drivers and hit-and-run vehicles**.

**Applications are made through a lawyer and the lawyer's fee is paid by the Fund.** There is
no cost to the applicant.

**Documents needed**
- Police accident report
- Photographs of the damage
- Vehicle damage assessment
- Doctor's report if there is injury
- Any invoices relating to the loss

## Section — What we could not verify

**Eyebrow:** Gaps
**H2:** Three things we are not going to state

- **The penalty for driving uninsured.** Set by Article 17 of Chapter 333. The figures in that
  article are not legible in the public PDF of the law and we found no current figure in any
  other official source. Almost every number returned by a search for this belongs to another
  country's legislation. We would rather leave it blank.
- **No-claims discount rates.** See above — not published.
- **Whether a foreign driving licence affects your cover.** This matters to anyone who bought a
  vehicle here on a home-country licence, and we could not settle it. In a 2022 press report the
  president of the Rental Car Operators Union said that people without a TRNC licence are
  treated as unlicensed and *"insurance does not cover them"*; in the same report the Director
  of the Traffic Department said a licence is accepted for as long as the residence permit runs.
  *These are press statements, not official texts, and they contradict each other.* Driving
  without a licence is an exclusion in the general conditions, so the question is a real one.
  **Ask your insurer in writing before you rely on the answer.**

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**Does this policy cover damage to my own car?**
No. It pays only for damage you cause to third parties. For your own vehicle you need
comprehensive motor insurance (kasko).

**How do I find the cheapest policy?**
Because a floor tariff applies, companies cannot go below the set minimum, so price differences
are narrow. It is more useful to compare how you reach the company when you have a claim, its
agency network and where its offices are.

**I drove in on a foreign-plated vehicle. Am I covered?**
No. A policy issued elsewhere is not valid here. Temporary Cyprus Motor Insurance is sold at the
port and land entry points. That is a different product from the annual policy on this page.

**Can I buy the policy online?**
Ten of the 29 companies issue a policy end to end online, six of them with an English interface.
Buying online does not change the price — the floor tariff applies whatever the channel.

**Where do I read the general conditions?**
KKSRSB publishes compulsory motor and kasko general conditions in Turkish and English. The
English text is official, not a translation.
