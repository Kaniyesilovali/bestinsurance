## Section — How to read this list

**H2:** What this list ranks

**Ranked by score, not by price.**
A floor tariff applies to compulsory motor insurance in the TRNC; companies cannot go below the
minimum premium set. So "cheapest" is not a ranking measure.

**A score is an observation, not a recommendation.**
A high score does not mean that a company will handle your claim well — because we cannot
measure claims payment performance. The score measures how visible a company is from the
outside.

**The list contains companies only.**
Agents and brokers are not here. The difference is explained below.

---

## Section — Company is not the same as agent

**H2:** Names you will not find in this list

Some names that appear in online lists of "TRNC insurance companies" are in fact **agents or
brokers**, not insurance companies and not members of KKSRSB. For example: **Azant Insurance**,
**Espada Sigorta**, **ESTA – North Cyprus Insurance Agency**.

**Why the difference matters:** who issues your policy, who carries the risk and who pays the
claim are the company's responsibility. An agent sells.

This is the second point most often confused in competing content.

## Section — Band legend

**H3:** Reading the bands

Beside each company name, eight bands show its score on the six measures. The order never
changes: transparency, product breadth, accessibility, digital service, foreign language,
corporate depth.

**The last two bands are empty for every company** — financial strength and claims payment
performance. These are areas we cannot measure, not shortcomings of the company.

## Section — Group affiliations

**H2:** Who is behind them

Some companies are the TRNC branch of a company from Türkiye; others are part of a banking
group. This is reflected in the corporate depth measure.

**TRNC branches of companies from Türkiye**
Anadolu Sigorta · AXA Sigorta · Zurich Sigorta · Türkiye Sigorta · GİG Sigorta ·
Anadolu Hayat Emeklilik

**Bank-affiliated companies**
Kıbrıs İktisat Sigorta (İktisatbank) · Creditwest Sigorta (Creditwest Bank) ·
Limasol Sigorta (Limasol Türk Kooperatif Bankası) · Near East (Near East group)

**Kıbrıs Sigorta Şirketi Ltd** has public company status; its largest shareholder is the TRNC
Development Bank with 37.06%.

**Warning — similar names:**
> Company names can mislead. We found no connection between **MAPFREE Insurance Ltd** and the
> Spanish **MAPFRE** group — the spellings differ as well. The names **London Insurance** and
> **Limasol Sigorta** carry a geographical suggestion, but no foreign group affiliation has been
> verified. If a name sounds familiar, that does not mean the company has anything to do with
> the institution it reminds you of.

## Section — Reinsurance

**H2:** The TRNC has its own reinsurance companies

Three of KKSRSB's 45 members are reinsurance companies: Alfa Reinsurance, Near East Sigorta,
Trust Capital Reinsurance. There are also three life and pension companies.

For a market of the TRNC's size, having its own local reinsurance companies is not usual.

**Note:** This list covers the 39 non-life companies. Life, pension and reinsurance companies
require different measures and will be treated separately.
