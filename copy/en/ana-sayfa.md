## Section — Not for EU readers to assume

**H2:** EU consumer protection does not apply here

If you are an expat, a student or an EU citizen, read this before you buy any policy in the
TRNC.

- **The Republic of Cyprus Financial Ombudsman has no jurisdiction in the TRNC.**
- **EU consumer-redress mechanisms do not apply in the TRNC.**
- **TRNC insurance companies are not covered by EU insurance schemes.**

In a dispute, the body you apply to is the Insurance Arbitration Commission within KKSRSB
(Kuzey Kıbrıs Sigorta ve Reasürans Şirketleri Birliği, the association of insurance and
reinsurance companies of Northern Cyprus).

## Section — The six measures

**H2:** Six measures, in the same order every time

**Lead:**
The order never changes. As you look down the list, each company forms its own silhouette —
and the last two bands are always empty. **What is empty is what we do not know, not what the
company is hiding.**

**Cards:**

**Transparency — 25%**
What the company publishes about itself: a corporate email address, an open postal address,
policy conditions.

**Product breadth — 20%**
How many classes of insurance the company actually offers products in.

**Accessibility — 20%**
Agency network, offices outside Lefkoşa, telephone and WhatsApp. How much use is a single
Lefkoşa office to someone living in Girne?

**Digital service — 20%**
Whether the website works and what you can do on it.

**Foreign language — 10%**
Whether the company serves readers in any language other than Turkish.

**Corporate depth — 5%**
Whether the company is part of a banking group or the branch of a company from Türkiye.

**Not scored — financial strength**
Not published company by company since 2016.

**Not scored — claims payment performance**
No ranking of paid claims was found for any year.

## Section — Compulsory motor insurance

**H2:** Compulsory third-party motor insurance

**Body:**
Every vehicle in the TRNC must carry compulsory third-party motor insurance under Chapter 333.
It pays for the damage you cause to the other party, not for damage to your own vehicle. The
sums insured differ from those in Türkiye: property damage 150,000 ₺, and death and injury up
to 8,000,000 ₺ on private vehicles. On commercial vehicles the death and injury limit is
15,000,000 ₺. **If the figure you are looking for is a Türkiye figure, it does not apply here.**

## Section — Types of insurance

**H2:** Types of insurance

**Cards:**

**Compulsory third-party motor insurance**
Compulsory for every vehicle. Covers the damage you cause to the other party.

**Comprehensive motor insurance (kasko)**
Optional. Covers damage to your own vehicle, including where you are at fault.

**Home insurance**
Earthquake, fire, flood and theft. Separate covers for owners and tenants.
**There is no compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC.**

**Health insurance**
The Health Fund premium is not a private policy. Which one you need depends on your residence
permit.

**Business insurance**
Commercial package, business interruption, liability covers, marine cargo and Contractors' All
Risks.

**Travel and border crossing insurance**
The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system. Crossing the border needs the other side's
own insurance.
