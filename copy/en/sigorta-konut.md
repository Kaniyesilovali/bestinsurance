## Intro

**Badge:** Not compulsory

**H1:** TRNC home insurance

**Intro text:**
It covers the structure of the home, its contents, or both. An owner and a tenant do not need
the same thing: one insures the building, the other insures the contents.

## Section — Earthquake (the most important information on the page, at the top)

**Eyebrow:** Frequently asked
**H2:** There is no compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC

In Türkiye, **DASK** is compulsory for homes. We could not identify any regulation
establishing an equivalent compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC.

What this means in practice: **if you have not bought earthquake cover, you do not have it.**
Do not expect a state umbrella to apply automatically. Earthquake is usually offered as one
cover item within a home package. Check whether it is written into your policy.

**Note box:**
Our legislative search did not establish whether any class of insurance other than motor is
legally compulsory in the TRNC. So when we say home insurance is "not compulsory", we are
saying that we found no compulsory requirement in the sources available to us. If we find a
regulation showing otherwise, we will correct this.

## Section — Cover

**Eyebrow:** Cover
**H2:** What is insured

**Building (structure)**
Walls, roof, installations, fitted cupboards. Usually the owner's policy.

**Contents**
Furniture, white goods, electronics, personal belongings. This is usually the tenant's policy.

**Typical covers**
- Fire and explosion
- Earthquake
- Flood and internal water damage
- Theft
- Glass breakage
- Storm

**Below the box:**
In the product ranges of TRNC companies, home package, glass breakage and theft also appear as
separate headings. Whether a cover is included in the package or sold as an add-on varies by
company.

## Section — Owner or tenant

**H2:** One home can carry two separate policies

| | Owner | Tenant |
|---|---|---|
| Damage to the building | Insures it | Does not insure it |
| Own contents | Optional | Insures them |
| Contents of a home they let out | May insure | — |
| Water damage caused to a neighbour | Check the liability cover | Check the liability cover |

**Below the table:**
If you are a tenant, the owner's policy does not cover your contents. If you are an owner, the
tenant's policy does not cover your building.

## Section — Before you buy

**H2:** Four questions before you sign the policy

1. **Is earthquake cover included?** If it is not written down, it is not there.
2. **How was the sum insured set?** The cost of rebuilding the home today and the price you
   paid for it are not the same thing. If the sum insured is too low, the claim may not be
   paid in full.
3. **What is the deductible?** The share you pay yourself on every claim.
4. **Is an unoccupied home excluded?** Policies may restrict cover for holiday homes or homes
   left empty for long periods.

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**I am a tenant. Do I need insurance?**
It is not a legal requirement. But the owner's policy does not cover your contents.

**Does the state pay for earthquake damage?**
We could not identify a compulsory earthquake insurance in the TRNC equivalent to DASK. How
state assistance works is a separate matter, and it does not replace insurance.

**Where can I read the general conditions?**
The KKSRSB general conditions page publishes the fire general conditions. There is no single
official general-conditions text covering home packages as a whole. Ask the company for its
own policy conditions.

**My home was bought with a loan. Is insurance compulsory?**
Not as a legal requirement, but the finance provider may require it under the contract.
