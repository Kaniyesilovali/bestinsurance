## Section — Green Card (at the top, in the warning colour)

**Badge:** Common mistake
**H2:** The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system

This one sentence answers most questions:

- **Green Card policies issued in Türkiye or in the EU are not valid in the TRNC.**
- **Green Cards are not issued for TRNC-registered vehicles.**

So if you are crossing a border with your vehicle — in either direction — you must buy that
side's own insurance.

---

## Section — Which situation are you in

**H2:** Three situations, three separate answers

**You are arriving from Türkiye by car**
You must take out **Temporary Cyprus Motor Insurance** (Geçici Kıbrıs Trafik Sigortası) at the
point of entry. Your Turkish policy and your Green Card are not valid.

**You are crossing from the south to the north (foreign or EU plates)**
TRNC compulsory third-party insurance is bought from the **"Insurance / Sigorta"** kiosks in
the buffer zone.

**You are crossing to the south with a TRNC-registered vehicle**
Your TRNC policy is not valid in the south. Separate insurance is required at the crossing
point.

**Box — the rule works both ways:**
> Insurance bought in the south is not valid in the north, and insurance bought in the north
> is not valid in the south.
> Neither side recognises the other's policy.

## Section — Practical detail (the most useful part of the page)

**H2:** What to know before you go to the gate

**Only compulsory insurance is sold**
**Kasko is not sold at the gate.** You can buy only compulsory third-party liability
insurance. If you are crossing in a rental car, ask the rental company whether the vehicle's
kasko is valid on the other side of the border.

**The gate is open 24/7; the insurance desk is not**
The crossing points are open at night as well. But **insurance is sold only between roughly
09:00 and 17:00** (10:00–18:00 at some gates). If you cross in the evening you may not be able
to buy insurance.

**Not every gate sells insurance**

| Gate | Insurance sold |
|---|---|
| Metehan (Ayios Dometios) | Yes |
| Derinya | Yes |
| Beyarmudu (Pergamos / Pyla) | Yes |
| Strovilia | Yes |
| Güzelyurt (Astromeritis / Zodhia) | Yes |
| Pirgos | Yes |
| **Lefke** | **No** |
| Ledra Palace | Pedestrians and bicycles only |
| Lokmacı | Pedestrians and bicycles only |

**Below the table:**
Vehicles do not cross at Ledra Palace or Lokmacı. If you are crossing at Lefke you must arrange
your insurance elsewhere.

## Section — Travel health insurance

**Eyebrow:** Abroad
**H2:** Leaving the TRNC for another country

Travel health insurance is required for visa applications. What to check when buying a policy:

- **Geographical scope** — a Schengen application generally requires 30,000 € of cover and
  cover across the whole Schengen area
- **Where the policy was issued** — some consulates raise problems with policies issued in the
  TRNC; ask the consulate before you apply
- Cover for treatment costs, medical evacuation and repatriation of remains

**Note:**
Which policy is accepted for a visa varies by consulate, and there is no central TRNC source
that can confirm this. Get the definite answer from the consulate you are applying to.

## Section — Frequently asked questions

**I have a Green Card. Is it valid in the TRNC?**
No. The TRNC is not a member of the Green Card system.

**Can I buy kasko at the gate?**
No, only compulsory third-party insurance is sold.

**I am crossing at night. Can I buy insurance?**
The gates stay open but insurance sales close in the evening. Plan to cross during the day.

**Is my TRNC policy valid in the south?**
No. A separate policy is required in each direction.

**Can I cross the border in a rental car?**
Rental agreements may restrict this. Ask the rental company first; a separate tariff applies to
rental cars at the gate.
